# Tata Steel Ltd — Equity Research Report

**Tata Steel Ltd | NSE: TATASTEEL | BSE: 500470 | Price: INR 148 as of 12 Mar 2025 | Market Cap: INR 1,84,000 Cr | Rating: BUY | Target Price: INR 175 (+18% upside)**

---

## Investment Summary

Tata Steel is India's largest steel producer with ~35 MTPA domestic capacity (post-Kalinganagar Phase 2 ramp-up) and ~10 MTPA in Europe. We recommend **BUY** driven by domestic volume growth, improving European operations post restructuring, and an expected recovery in steel realisations over the next 12 months. The stock trades at a meaningful discount to its historical mid-cycle multiples.

## Recommendation

**BUY** — 12-month target price of **INR 175**, derived from a blended 60% DCF / 40% EV/EBITDA peer comparison.

## Key Investment Highlights

- **Volume growth:** Kalinganagar Phase 2 expansion (5 MTPA) is ramping up, expected to add ~INR 8,000 Cr incremental revenue at steady state, lifting India EBITDA margins to ~23–25%.
- **European turnaround:** UK operations have been restructured under a JV with the UK government (£500 mn support); transition to an electric arc furnace lowers unit costs and carbon exposure, improving European break-even by ~£60/t by FY27E.
- **Raw-material advantage:** ~70% captive iron-ore sourcing in India insulates margins from global ore-price volatility; coking-coal procurement diversification continues to reduce input costs.

## Key Risks

- **Steel cyclicality:** A prolonged demand slowdown in China or India infrastructure spending cuts could depress HRC prices below INR 48,000/t, compressing margins.
- **High net debt:** Consolidated net debt remains elevated at ~INR 77,000 Cr (FY25E); rising rates or cost overruns on Kalinganagar could delay deleveraging.
- **Regulatory/FX risk:** EU CBAM compliance costs and INR/GBP volatility may adversely impact European profitability.
